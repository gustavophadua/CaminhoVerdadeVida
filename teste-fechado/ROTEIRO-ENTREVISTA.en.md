# Interview script

**Português:** [ROTEIRO-ENTREVISTA.md](ROTEIRO-ENTREVISTA.md). This is a translation; if the two differ, the Portuguese
text prevails.

Proposal of 2026-10-30, adapted from Delivery C, section 24. Audience not yet chosen; two versions. There has been no interview or contact. Ask for authorization, use public/fictional examples and avoid third parties' data. Do not ask for passwords, keys or private content. Price will be a later concrete proposal, not a promise during recruitment.

## People

1. Tell us about the last time a conclusion seemed convincing, but you noticed a link was missing. What was the task?

2. How much time or effort did it take to check? Tell us without exposing private information.

3. How often does this happen? Are there examples that worked out and others that failed, which you authorize us to use?

4. How do you check today: rereading, another person, a second chat, rules? What works and what is still hard?

5. What do you call correct: a true fact, a faithful interpretation, a logical consequence or good writing?

6. Which texts would you not send to the cloud? Would you need fully local use?

7. Would an inconclusive result with the missing hypothesis help? When does review start to be more work?

8. Do you decide about the tool and the payment, or does someone else decide? Do you already pay for some alternative?

9. After a pilot and a concrete proposal, what would be your next purchase step or reason to decline?

10. What would make you come back after a month? What would make you abandon it even after a good demonstration?

## Teams that build AI assistants

1. Tell us about the last time an AI answer seemed correct, but the conclusion was not supported. What task was being carried out and who noticed?

2. How much work did it take to check or fix? Is there a record of time, rework or consequence, without exposing confidential data?

3. How often does this problem appear? In how many recent cases did it not appear? Can we examine authorized representative examples, including the ones that worked out?

4. How do you check today: a person, a second model, tests, rules, an evaluation platform? What works and what is still hard?

5. When you say "correct", do you mean factual truth, faithful interpretation, compliance with a rule, logical consequence or quality of writing?

6. Which data cannot leave the environment? Who approves tooling, processing and retention? Is local installation required?

7. Would an inconclusive result accompanied by a missing hypothesis be useful? At what point does it stop saving work?

8. Who uses it, who pays and who can block the purchase? Which budget or existing tool would fund this?

9. After observing the pilot and its cost, would you accept a proposal with this scope and price? If not, what is the concrete obstacle and the next decision step?

10. What would need to happen for you to keep using it after a month? What result would make you cancel, even if the initial demonstration was good?

Record the recent episode, current alternative, frequency, the person responsible for the decision and a verifiable next step. Do not count praise or intention as payment; do not collect confidential material. Do not start collecting without a person responsible and defined privacy conditions.
