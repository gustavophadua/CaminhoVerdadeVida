# External set protocol

**Português:** [PROTOCOLO-CONJUNTO-EXTERNO.md](PROTOCOLO-CONJUNTO-EXTERNO.md). This is a translation; if the two differ,
the Portuguese text prevails.

Proposal of 2026-10-30. Not carried out. Collection and use with participants depend on the person responsible and on the privacy review.

## Collection

Collect 150 arguments written by other adults, without using a model to draft them. Obtain specific authorization for evaluation; authorization for publication or training is separate and optional. Do not ask for real names, third parties' data or sensitive information. A participant can decline or withdraw material according to the conditions given. Do not promise absolute anonymity of free text.

Seek variation of subject, length, schooling and structures (quantifiers, negation, necessary/sufficient condition, pronouns and fragments). Record the recruitment criteria and refusals. Do not claim statistical representativeness from a convenience sample.

## Review

Two people review each argument independently, without seeing Cosmos's translation or each other's annotation. They record the split into premises/conclusion, vocabulary, formulas, ambiguities and the class only when there is enough interpretation and a consistent base. Do not force a class on the ambiguous ones.

Compare the annotations. A disagreement of meaning goes to the author when authorized, or to a third reviewer; preserve alternatives if there is no resolution. Only then compare the formulas with the solver. Equivalence between two formulas does not guarantee faithfulness to the text. Publish agreement and the amount adjudicated, without personal data.

## Families and custody

Group arguments by logical structure and paraphrase before partitioning: members of the same family cannot fall into different training and test sets. Generate a manifest with ids, family, version and hash. The key between authorization and material stays separate, in the custody of a person designated by Gustavo. The reserved set is not accessible to whoever tunes the prompt. No historical closed file is reused here.

## Measurements

Proposal: one primary measurement after freezing model, prompt, code and criteria; a final confirmation only with a new recorded decision by Gustavo. Every attempt counts, including incomplete ones, with the reason and number of items. Do not look at the test's errors to tune the same version. If the items are opened for diagnosis, reclassify the batch as development and collect a new independent test.

Report faithfulness, class, false "proved" on negatives, no class, coverage, abstention, time and cost, with denominators and intervals where applicable. Publish only material with specific permission. This proposal does not start recruitment, collection or measurement.
