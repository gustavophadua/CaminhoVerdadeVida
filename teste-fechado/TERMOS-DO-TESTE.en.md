prevails.

1. **What it is.** Cosmos is software in a testing phase that translates lines of reasoning written in Portuguese into
   formal logic and checks, with an automatic verifier (Z3), whether the conclusion follows from the premises. The test
   is closed, by invitation.
2. **No warranty.** The software can make mistakes, including when reading the text. The results show the logical form
   of an argument; **they are not legal, financial, medical, psychological or professional advice**, nor do they state
   whether a premise is true in the world. Do not use Cosmos as the only basis for important decisions.
3. **Use.** The tester uses Cosmos on their own computer, for evaluation, without reselling it, redistributing it under
   another name or trying to get around the account's protections. The code is open under the repository's licenses
   (AGPL-3.0+ and CC BY 4.0).
4. **What the tester provides.** Only what they decide to send: exported error reports and comments. By sending a
   report, the tester authorizes the use of that content to fix Cosmos and to train the project's own model, without
   identifying who sent it. Do not send texts with third parties' personal data, company secrets or sensitive
   information.
5. **Test coin.** Parts of the software mention a **test** network and units. They are not the Cosmos coin, have no
   value, are not sold and give no right to anything in the future.
6. **Ending.** The test can be ended or changed at any time. The tester can stop whenever they want and delete the
   Cosmos folder and the browser data.
7. **Privacy.** The processing of data is in the test's Privacy Notice (`PRIVACIDADE.md`, in English
   `PRIVACIDADE.en.md`).
