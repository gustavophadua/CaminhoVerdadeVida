# Protocol for understanding the result

**Português:** [PROTOCOLO-COMPREENSAO.md](PROTOCOLO-COMPREENSAO.md). This is a translation; if the two differ, the
Portuguese text prevails.

Proposal of 2026-10-30. A 20-minute session with an adult and authorized fictional examples. Not carried out. It is not an evaluation of the person's intelligence or reliability.

1. Minutes 0-2: explain the purpose and the authorization, allow withdrawal; record only a research identifier. Recording requires separate consent.
2. Minutes 2-6: show a valid case (every A is B; a is A; therefore a is B). Ask the person to explain "follows" in their own words, without teaching the answer beforehand.
3. Minutes 6-10: show factually invented premises that support a conclusion. Ask whether the seal proves that the premises are true in the world and what would need to be checked.
4. Minutes 10-15: present a labeled study prototype with an intentional error in the "entendi" (I understood) reading: the text "some A is B" interpreted as "every A is B". Ask the person to locate the error and explain the correction. Do not insert an error into production code or packages.
5. Minutes 15-18: show a countermodel, an inconclusive result and a missing certificate. Ask the person to tell the three apart and describe the next step.
6. Minutes 18-20: reveal the deliberate error, clarify the limits and ask which part caused confusion. Allow answers to be withdrawn.

## Measurement

Rubric set before the session: 0 = incorrect; 1 = partial; 2 = correct, on each dimension: consequence under premises; factual truth kept separate; reviewable translation; meaning of inconclusive; scope of the certificate. Two reviewers code the answers independently and preserve disagreements.

Record spontaneous detection of the wrong translation, time to detection, need for help and confusion between seal and truth. Do not count an answer after instruction as spontaneous understanding. Report counts and denominators, including withdrawals and interrupted sessions. Acceptance criteria will be set by the person responsible before starting, according to the audience; do not invent an observed success percentage.

Keep the answers for the period stated in the consent, with access control and deletion. Do not send transcripts to an external model without further authorization. Study material does not automatically go into training.
