## 4. The physics already explained (reference)

### 4.1 Newton

- **Perspective:** absolute space and time, from outside any body. For him this was linked to God's omnipresence (General Scholium, 1713). It is the description closest to Alpha's view: without an internal observer.
- **State and law:** the state is the position and velocity of each body. Force changes the quantity of motion (the form F = m·a is Euler's, around 1750). Forces come in equal and opposite pairs. Gravity is F = G·m₁·m₂/r² and acts instantaneously.
- **Determinism:** the initial state plus the force law fix the whole past and the whole future (Laplace).
- **What is detectable:** uniform velocity is not (Corollary V). Acceleration and rotation are (the bucket argument).
- **What he took into account:** Galileo (fall, projectile, inertia), Descartes (inertia in a straight line), Kepler (ellipses, areas, T² ∝ r³), Huygens, Wallis and Wren (v²/r and collisions), Hooke (decomposing the orbit), measurements of the Moon, the tides and pendulums.
- **The deduction:** the law of areas implies a central force. An ellipse implies 1/r². The same constant for all planets implies a force proportional to mass, and the 3rd law gives F ∝ M·m. The Moon test: at 60 Earth radii the acceleration is 0.00272 m/s², equal to g/60².
- **Limits:** it fails near the speed of light, in strong gravity and at the atomic scale. It does not explain the mechanism of gravity.

### 4.2 Einstein

- **Special relativity (1905):** the laws are the same in every inertial frame and the speed of light is the same for everyone. Consequences: simultaneity is relative, moving clocks mark less time (Δt = γ·Δτ), the interval s² = c²Δt² − Δx² is the same for everyone, and each event is only influenced by what is in its past light cone.
- **General relativity (1915):** free fall is locally indistinguishable from the absence of gravity. Gravity is geometry: free bodies follow geodesics in a curved spacetime. Equation: G_μν = (8πG/c⁴)·T_μν. The geometry is dynamic and its changes travel at c. Newton reappears as a limiting case.

### 4.3 Tesla

Engineering of oscillating fields: the rotating magnetic field (induction motor), alternating-current transmission (loss P = I²·R), resonance (f = 1/(2π√(LC))) and the attempt at wireless energy. He produced no theory of fields, of space or of gravity.

### 4.4 Quantum mechanics

- **What it took into account:** thermal radiation (Planck), the photoelectric effect, stable atoms and spectra (Bohr), matter waves (λ = h/p), the double slit with one particle at a time.
- **State:** an amplitude ψ(x, t) for each position. It is not position and velocity.
- **Evolution:** the Schrödinger equation, iħ·∂ψ/∂t = Ĥψ. It is deterministic and linear.
- **Measurement:** a single result, with probability |ψ|². Afterwards, ψ starts again from the result.
- **Uncertainty:** Δx·Δp ≥ ħ/2. Newton's initial state (exact x and v) does not exist.
- **Composite systems:** a single ψ for the whole. In entanglement, the whole has a definite state and each part, on its own, does not.
- **Open:** the measurement problem and the union with gravity.

## 5. Where the model meets physics

**Coincides without analogy:**

- "Alpha knows the initial value and the mechanisms, so the line simply is" is Newton's and Laplace's determinism. But determined is not the same as straight: straight only holds without force. In Einstein, the straight line becomes a geodesic.
- "For Alpha there is no will be" corresponds to Einstein's spacetime: the history of a body is a whole curve (world line), and the passage is the proper time along it. This reading (block universe) is an interpretation, not an experimental result.
- "The fragment only sees its value" has a rigorous version: the past light cone.
- In quantum mechanics, a measurement delivers one value and ψ cannot be read in a single measurement. In entanglement, the whole has complete information and the part, incomplete.
- The sum of two waves in opposition is zero all the time. That is Alpha's line in the DNA graph.
- A helix seen from the side is a wave, seen from the front it is a circle and seen from inside it is a spiral. The DNA, the clock and the spiral are the same Tube.
- "The Whole's energy is preserved" (A.14) is the first law, and the machine already obeys it: reserve + motion + spent = total (proved, `confere-z3.py`, items 7 to 9). "Having energy to spend" is free energy, which only goes down (second law). The two hold together, and conservation alone does not decide the spending: spending is one more law, it "only adds" (in Cosmos, tubo-6, D3 and D4).
- In deep sleep, the cortex's long-range communication breaks down (Massimini and others, 2005) and the hippocampus replays, compressed in time, sequences of what was lived (in rats: Wilson and McNaughton, 1994; Nádasdy and others, 1999). Dreams bring isolated pieces, almost never a whole episode (Fosse and others, 2003). That is A.14's "closes the observer point and goes back to what already collided, blurry", in deep sleep. In REM sleep the point does not close completely (there is dreaming), but the dorsolateral prefrontal cortex, which checks, is among the least active areas (Maquet and others, 1996). Details and sources in `leis-da-fisica.en.md`, section 6.
- "Matter swallows matter" (A.14): gravity gathers matter. In a merger, mass and momentum are conserved, and the heat ½·μ·(Δv)² is never negative (tubo-6, A2 to A4).

**It is an analogy when applied to people:** curvature of spacetime, entanglement and any quantum effect. In large, hot bodies, interaction with the environment destroys entanglement almost immediately. So is the observer point: in the machine it is a point that walks on the perimeter; the correspondence with a person's sleep is structural, not a measurement of the machine.

**Contradicts or needs a decision:**

- "Alpha knows the value" requires the value to exist before measurement. Bell's theorem and the experiments exclude pre-existing, local values. Four ways out remain: the value does not exist before measuring (Copenhagen), the value exists but is non-local (Bohm), all results happen (Everett), or everything was fixed at the beginning, including the choice of what to measure (superdeterminism, the closest to the model and the least accepted).
- The real annihilation of matter and antimatter turns into light, and the universe was left with a small excess of matter, about 1 part in 1 billion. Equal levels oscillating forever is a postulate of the model.
- From inside a tube, the helix appears as a hyperbolic spiral (the size falls with distance), not as Fibonacci's.
- "Hotter, more collisions" is kinetic theory (Boltzmann), not quantum mechanics.
- Spent energy does not disappear: it becomes heat.
- In image 2, the pairs of numbers repeat at different dots, so they are not "distinct locations".
- In image 3, "S₁ × time" is a cylinder. For it to be a cone, the radius needs to be a function of time. The machine now gives that function (section 6.1).
- The concentrated end (Omega point, A.14) only happens in a universe that contracts again. With constant dark energy there is no collapse: the end is cold and uniform, as the Principia says (page 5). DESI's 2025 data prefer a dark energy that changes with time (2.8 to 4.2σ, depending on the supernovae used), and a 2025 fit gives a collapse in about 20 billion years; none of this is confirmed (D39).
- In general relativity, the total energy of an expanding universe has no defined global conservation, only local: light loses energy as it is stretched with space. "The Whole's energy is preserved" holds without caveat in Newton, in special relativity and in quantum mechanics, and only locally at the scale of the universe.
- Sleep is not rest in the sense of T04 (A.6): in Cosmos, "some human sleeps and rests" contradicts the base (tubo-5, S4). The sleeping brain keeps spending: in deep sleep, 12% to 44% less glucose than awake, depending on the study; in REM, almost the same (D36).

1. At the beginning, matter and antimatter have the same level, the highest.
2. The two stay in opposition. The sum is zero: Alpha's line.
3. The DNA, the clock and the spiral are the same Tube seen from the side, from the front and from inside.
4. A slice is cut each time matter and antimatter cross: two per turn of the clock.
5. The observers travel along the perimeter of the slice, at rest or with force.
6. When two observers meet, they collide. Each collision spends energy.
7. The more energy spent, the smaller the radius of the Tube. When the energy runs out, the Tube closes.

**Suppositions the machine needed (all pending the author's confirmation):**

- S1. Angle of the clock: θ = 360° × t ÷ T. Matter is at θ and antimatter at θ + 180°, rotating in the same direction.
- S2. Level in the DNA: matter = r·cos θ, antimatter = r·cos(θ + 180°), where r is the relative radius of the Tube.
- S3. The energy an observer uses to accelerate comes out of the Whole's reserve.
- S4. Force: whoever is "with force" accelerates a = F ÷ m in their own direction up to cruising speed. Whoever is "at rest" exerts no force and only changes speed through collision.
- S5. A collision is a meeting: two observers pass through the same point of the perimeter. The quantity of motion is conserved, the relative velocity is multiplied by e = √(1 − loss), and each goes on their way. Energy spent = (m ÷ 4) × loss × (relative velocity)².
- S6. Radius = initial radius × √(energy not yet spent ÷ total energy). This is the relation of an oscillator: energy grows with the square of the amplitude.
- S7. Neighbors start in opposite directions, equally spaced, at rest speed.
- S8. End: when 1 % of the energy is left, or when the reserve has run out and the observers no longer meet.
- S9. "n• = 60 s" was read as the duration of one turn of the clock. Hence n = v × T ÷ perimeter: turns around the perimeter per turn of the clock.
- S10. (proposed on 2026-11-10, from the author's answer) The loss per collision is never zero. With this, "every collision spends energy" becomes a proved rule (items 24 and 25). With zero loss the machine makes 1,797 collisions in 600 s and spends 0 J.

- S17 to S20. (proposed on 2026-11-11, from A.14; none of them is in the machine) S17: two fragments that meet with total loss become one, with the sum of the masses and the velocity of the center of mass (D41).
