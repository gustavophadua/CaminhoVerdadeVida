"Pi" has two senses here, and both touch the Tube.

## 1. The number π: it is neither matter nor antimatter; it is the turn that takes one into the other

In the model, antimatter sits 180° from matter, and 180° is π radians. In Euler's form:

- e^{iπ} = −1: turning by π transforms matter into antimatter, (c, s) → (−c, −s) (proved, A1).
- e^{iπ} + 1 = 0: matter added to matter turned by π gives zero. It is Alpha's line (proved, A2).
- π is the only turn that, done twice, returns to the start without being the null turn (proved, A3). The antimatter of antimatter is matter. In physics, charge conjugation C has exactly this property (C² = 1).

A number is not a substance. But, in the Tube, π is the operation that separates the two, and e^{iπ} + 1 = 0 is the equation of the DNA in one line.

## 2. The pion (the particle π): yes, literally

| Particle | Made of | Charge | Baryon number | Antiparticle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| π⁺ | u d̄ (a quark and an antiquark) | +1 | 0 | π⁻ |
| π⁻ | d ū | −1 | 0 | π⁺ |
| π⁰ | superposition of u ū and d d̄ | 0 | 0 | itself |

- **The three pions have matter and antimatter inside:** a quark and an antiquark each.
- **The π⁰ is its own antiparticle.** Swapping matter for antimatter (u ↔ ū, d ↔ d̄) gives back the same state. Only something with all charges equal to zero can be like this (proved, C1: q = −q forces q = 0). Other examples: the photon and the Z boson.
- **In physics' accounting, the pion is "neither one nor the other":** the baryon number, which measures the universe's excess of matter, is 1/3 − 1/3 = 0. The matter and antimatter inside it cancel out in the count.

Masses and lifetimes (reference values from the Particle Data Group): π± ≈ 139.57 MeV/c², lives about 2.6 × 10⁻⁸ s; π⁰ ≈ 134.98 MeV/c², lives about 8.4 × 10⁻¹⁷ s. Predicted by Yukawa (1935); the charged ones were found in cosmic rays in 1947 and the neutral one in 1950.

## 3. The π⁰ becomes light, at 180°

- About 98.8 % of the time, the π⁰ decays into two photons.
- At rest, each photon carries half the mass (67.49 MeV) and they leave in opposite directions, at 180° (proved, D1).
- It never decays into three photons: the π⁰ has C parity equal to +1, each photon has −1, and three photons would give −1 (forbidden; experiments limit it to less than 3 in 100 million).

The π⁰ is the closest real example of what the notebook describes, at the scale of a particle. The parallel is structural: it does not show that the Tube exists. And there is a difference: in the π⁰ the coexistence lasts a tiny fraction of a second; in the model, the DNA oscillates until the Tube closes.

## 5. What physics does not know yet

Whether the neutrino is its own antiparticle (a Majorana particle) is open. The search is for neutrinoless double beta decay; none has been seen yet. If it is, the neutrino would be another case of matter and antimatter at the same time, and could help explain the universe's excess of matter (E3 in `solucoes.md`).
